The House

Mee Aakruthi — A vision, tailored to you.

Mee Aakruthi was founded with a simple belief: that exceptional clothing begins with exceptional attention to detail. Built by a trained fashion designer with a deep appreciation for craft, the house was created to bring the quality and personal attention of a designer studio to every client. Every garment is developed in-house, from the first sketch and selection of textile to cutting, fitting, and finishing. Each piece is thoughtfully shaped around the individual.

Whether creating a statement piece for a special occasion or refining an everyday silhouette, we believe good design should feel personal. It should reflect the person who wears it.
