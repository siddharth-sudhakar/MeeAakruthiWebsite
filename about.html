The house

Aakruthi — the form a garment takes.

MeeAakruthi began as a studio practice: drafting on the stand, working with Indian textiles and contemporary cuts, and building clothes that feel considered rather than seasonal.

The Fashion Designer takes private commissions. Every piece is cut in-house, fitted on the body, and finished by hand.
