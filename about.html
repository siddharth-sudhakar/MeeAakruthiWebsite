The House

Mee Aakruthi - a vision, tailored to you.

Mee Aakruthi was founded with a simple belief: exceptional clothing begins with exceptional attention to detail. Built by a trained fashion designer with a deep appreciation for craft, the house brings the quality and personal attention of a designer studio to every client.

Every garment is developed in-house, from the first sketch and textile selection to cutting, fitting, and finishing. Whether creating a statement piece for a special occasion or refining an everyday silhouette, good design should feel personal.
